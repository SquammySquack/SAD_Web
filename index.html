Features

Why upgrade to the DCM & Co. system?

Everything your audit team needs to detect, prioritize, and report risk in one place.

Cross-Client Fraud Analytics

Breaks down data silos by cross-matching vendor details such as TINs and bank accounts across clients to flag ghost vendors instantly.

Machine Learning Risk Scoring

Replace random sampling. Historical data drives High, Medium, or Low risk scores so you know exactly where to audit first.

Smart Receipt Processing (OCR)

Upload receipts and invoices and the system extracts vendors, dates, and amounts, then flags duplicates and missing requirements.

Smart Spending Tracker

Automatically categorize client expenses and monitor spending over time to spot sudden spikes and unusual patterns.

Real-Time Alerts & Reports

Generate automated Fraud and Anomaly Reports summarizing red flags, so you act now instead of discovering issues months later.

Centralized Client Database

Replace scattered folders and spreadsheets. Documents, transaction histories, and engagement statuses live in one secure dashboard.

How it works

From upload to insight in four steps

A simple workflow that fits into your existing engagement process.

Upload documents

Add receipts, invoices, and transaction records for each client.

Extract & categorize

OCR reads the details and expenses are sorted automatically.

Analyze & score

Cross-client matching and ML models flag anomalies and rate risk.

Review & report

Prioritize high-risk clients and export a fraud and anomaly report.
